Let's Refill! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Developer: Vanguard Forge Studios · Contact: hq.vanguardforge@gmail.com

Let's Refill! ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android made by Vanguard Forge Studios. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you; the ads in it come from Google AdMob, which collects some information to show and measure ads, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

What the game collects

Nothing itself. The game has no accounts and no sign-in. It doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward, and a short ad between levels (which "Remove Ads" turns off). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob's software in the game collects information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with the ads. Google describes this at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and in Google's privacy policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent with Google's own consent form, and SETTINGS has a PRIVACY CHOICES button to change it at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are made through Google Play. The game never sees your payment details; Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can give it to you.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your own phone so it can work:

  • your progress, coins, streaks and settings (sound, music and vibration)
  • a small log of what happens in play, such as levels started and finished, help used, and any error messages, kept to help find problems while the game is being tested

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Sharing

When you tap SHARE after Today's Pour, your phone's own share menu opens with a short message about the day's colour. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with, and what happens next is up to that app.

Permissions

  • Vibration, for the game's haptic feedback. You can turn it off in the game's settings.
  • Notifications, only if you say yes when the game asks, for at most one reminder a day (for example that Today's Pour is ready). They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in the game's settings or your phone's settings. So that reminders survive a restart, the game also asks to run briefly when the phone starts up.
  • Internet access, for the ads, and for Google Play to process purchases.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID), used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Google Play billing, so purchases can be made through Google Play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds analytics or changes what is collected, this policy will be updated to say exactly what is collected and why, before that version is released. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
