Wobblo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Developer: Vanguard Forge Studios · Contact: [YOUR EMAIL]

Wobblo is a puzzle game for children and families. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no chat, no sign-in, and no in-app purchases. The only network use is Google's ads: a short ad between some games, and optional ads the player taps to watch.

Information we collect

None. We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information from players. The game keeps its progress (scores, stars, unlocked jellies, settings) only on your device. We cannot see it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Wobblo shows a short ad between games (after every few finished games, never during a game, and never in a new player's first two games), and offers optional rewarded ads: a player can tap to watch a short ad for extra stars, a bonus gift or another go. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Every ad request from Wobblo is marked as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, and limited to content rated G. This means Google shows only non-personalised ads, and does not use the information for interest-based advertising or remarketing.

Wobblo does not use the Android Advertising ID: the permission for it is removed from the app. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software may process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address (which can suggest a general location), the app set ID, basic device and app information, ad interactions (for example that an ad was shown or tapped), and performance diagnostics. This information goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

Wobblo is designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (or anyone), and the game has no way for a child to share personal information with us or with other players. Wobblo is made to follow Google Play's Families policy.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load the optional ads.
  • Vibration: for small vibrations during play (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by Google's ads software for its own background work.

Deleting your data

All game progress is stored on your device. Uninstalling Wobblo deletes it. You can also reset the best score in the game's Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
